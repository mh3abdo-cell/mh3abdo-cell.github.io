Overview

Letter Bubbles: ABC & 123 (package com.mrmstfa.letterbubbles) is a learning game for children aged 3–8, developed by Mostafa Hussein (Mr Mstfa). It is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, sign-up, chat, social features, location access, camera or microphone access. Game progress is stored only on the device and never sent to us.

Advertising

The app may show ads through Google AdMob. All ad requests are flagged as child-directed (tagForChildDirectedTreatment), limited to the G (general audiences) content rating, and are non-personalized: no interest-based advertising, no remarketing, and no Advertising ID is used for profiling. Only ad networks certified for Google Play's Families program are used. Google may process limited technical data (such as IP address and device type) solely to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, as required by law for child-directed services. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party links

Any link leaving the app (for example to a store page) is placed behind a parental gate.

Parents' rights

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to review or delete. If you believe information about your child has been provided to us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.
